# Zone 3 — Mt. Olympus

**19 actions.** High-tier magic, soulstone investment, artifact discovery and permanent buffs precede a reputation-dependent branch.

## Gameplay dependencies

- `Climb Mountain` and `Explore Cavern` unlock magic/stone progression. `Mana Geyser`, `Decipher Runes`, `Chronomancy`, `Looping Potion` and `Pyromancy` affect resource production and skill growth.
- `Mine Soulstones` is limited and requires a pickaxe, plus Cavern progress; soulstones improve stat experience and are spent by rituals/imbuement. `Check Walls` and `Take Artifacts` feed later artifact trade.
- `Imbue Mind` requires Illusions ≥70, Magic ≥300 and sacrificial soulstones; `Imbue Body` requires mind imbuement. Both are multipart and can grant persistent buffs.
- `RuinsZ3` / `HaulZ3` supply another tower stone origin.

## Travel and checks

`Face Judgement` can travel **3→4** at reputation **>50** or **3→5** at reputation **≤50**. Exactly 50 goes to Startington because the `<=50` branch runs first. `Guru` is an alternate **3→4** route requiring 1,000 herbs and global Explore ≥100. Test the exact reputation boundary, remaining soulstones, buff caps, artifact discovery and travel predictions.
