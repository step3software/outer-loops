# Zone 2 — Merchanton

**20 actions.** Commerce, guild ranks and group combat become central.

## Gameplay dependencies

- `Explore City` feeds `Gamble`, `Get Drunk`, `Sell Potions` and the wider economy. `Buy Mana Z3` converts all carried gold to mana, with a special `portalUsed` restriction.
- `Adventure Guild` and `Crafting Guild` are multipart paths with a shared `guild === ""` start gate. Their segment/rank bonuses influence combat, armor/crafting and available plans. `Gather Team` and `Large Dungeon` couple group resources with Combat/Magic.
- `Craft Armor`, `Apprentice`, `Mason` and `Architect` extend the crafting/building chain. `Buy Pickaxe` gates later soulstone mining.
- `Heroes Trial` is a 50-floor trial granting Heroism-related progression.

## Travel and checks

`Start Trek` advances **2→3**, visible at City ≥30 and unlocked at City ≥60. `Underworld` advances **2→7**, requiring 500 gold and global Explore ≥50, a later shortcut rather than the initial route. Test guild exclusivity, rank scaling, buy/sell chains, team strength, dungeon floor rewards, tools and route legality.
