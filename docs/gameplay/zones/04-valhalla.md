# Zone 4 — Valhalla

**25 actions.** Social/favor networks, artifact trade, citizenship, magical ranks and combat buffs accumulate.

## Gameplay dependencies

- `Guided Tour`, `Canvass`, `Donate`, `Accept Donations` and `Tidy Up` produce and gate reputation and favors.
- `Sell Artifact` / `Gift Artifact` spend the artifact pipeline; `Buy Mana Z5` exchanges gold for mana, conditional on portal state.
- `Mercantilism` improves economic conversion; `Charm School` and `Oracle` supply training; `Wizard College` is a multi-segment rank system with gold/favor requirements and magic-related bonuses.
- `Seek Citizenship`, `Build Housing`, `Collect Taxes` and `Pegasus` form civic/economic goals. `Enchant Armor`, `Fight Frost Giants` and `Great Feast` expand combat and buff options, with `Seek Blessing` as another branch.

## Travel and checks

`Fall From Grace` takes the player **4→5**, requiring Pyromancy ≥200. Startington can also be reached directly through the other `Face Judgement` outcome. Check multi-resource prerequisites, wizard/frost rank effects, housing/taxes, artifact value, feast buff and route transitions.
