# RW02 — First Empowered Timeline and Safe Replay Transition

**Lifecycle:** Proposed milestone; no plan approval or implementation authorization.
**Owner origin:** Approved potion-first Real World direction and 2026-11-07 request for a complete Outer Loop interaction-to-rerun flow.
**Repository:** `step3software/outer-loops` at `main` revision `860872f7db08a1870d33f6105a5d9aae40ea5f18`.
**Dependencies:** [RW01-A](RW01-A.md), [RW01-B](RW01-B.md), and [RF02](../../refactoring/RF02-persistence-safety.md) recovery safeguards before destructive resets.

## Objective

After Outer Loop exploration and the first conversation, let the player **explicitly** start a new timeline with allocatable starting skill points, a major bounded temporary boost, permanent free approximately **10× replay throughput**, and an early meaningful durable ability choice, without losing valuable legacy progress.

## Product contracts and scope

- **Approved:** Drinking a potion, visiting the outer world, meeting Other You and committing to a new timeline are separate transitions. Entry itself does not reset the current timeline or award first-rerun grants.
- Build an explicit **Start New Timeline** contextual activity with a reset/retention preview, confirmation and an atomic/recoverable commit; preserve original/unfinished Valley state until commitment.
- Separate inner-loop, timeline, Outer Loop and UI-session lifetimes; persist timeline IDs, historical summaries, first-entry and one-time grant receipts in a versioned compatible save format.
- Award allocatable skill points and a clearly bounded major temporary boost under separately approved units, effect, duration and stacking contracts; do not silently reuse legacy prestige points as the new currency.
- Unlock free permanent approximately 10× **wall-clock** replay speed for eligible new timelines without changing comparable original in-world action costs, rewards, mana budgets or effective-time gates.
- Provide at least one meaningful enduring ability choice under stable ability IDs and safe migration/rollback, without assuming a permanent class lock or final tree taxonomy.
- Preserve Valley/`RestoreTime` and credits independently of potion entry; safely migrate/retain old prestige, challenge-active and historical saves, without two competing prestige shops at unified release.
- **Owner decisions still required:** point allocation (RW-Q28), boost effect/expiry (RW-Q29), speed stacking (RW-Q04), legacy point treatment (RW-Q02), timeline return/archive (RW-Q06), and tree taxonomy (RW-Q13). Proposed defaults cannot grant approval.

## Exclusions

No later RW03 automation, RW04 variants, RW05 parties, RW06 customized potions, RW07 wagers, RW08 gods-as-bosses, campaign-wide balance changes or unapproved economy conversions. No automatic new timeline on drinking or completing a conversation.

## Acceptance criteria

- **RW02-AC01:** Only explicit confirmation of Start New Timeline resets selected timeline state; potion entry and dialogue preserve the active original campaign.
- **RW02-AC02:** Cancelled/failed/interrupted/repeated commits recover the previous timeline without duplicate grants or partial irreversible reset.
- **RW02-AC03:** Outer Loop character and progression state persists through ordinary loops, timeline resets, reload/export/import and restores according to accepted lifetime contracts.
- **RW02-AC04:** First-rerun points are visibly allocatable via a validated UI and granted once under the accepted economy, distinct from old prestige points.
- **RW02-AC05:** The powerful temporary first-rerun boost has an observable approved effect, cap and expiry; it cannot persist or stack beyond contract.
- **RW02-AC06:** Approximately 10× free replay throughput improves wall-clock execution without changing corresponding action costs, mana, outcomes, effective-time checks or predictor semantics.
- **RW02-AC07:** At least one lasting ability choice is available without permanent class locking by assumption, and the released experience does not maintain a second independent prestige/challenge shop.
- **RW02-AC08:** Veteran saves, Valley `RestoreTime` history/credits, challenge-active saves and existing purchased effects remain valid or have separately approved recoverable migration.
- **RW02-AC09:** Browser, model and predictor worker parity tests cover normal/new timeline reset, rewards, boost expiry, offline time, save-reload and error recovery.
