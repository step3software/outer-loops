# RW08 — Incremental Timeline Bosses

**Status:** Proposed milestone; owner-confirmed concept, detailed mechanics not approved.
**Repository:** `step3software/outer-loops`.
**Owner origin:** 2026-11-07 owner discussion: the Valley of Olympus fights against the gods are an opportunity for timeline bosses, which **must be solved incrementally**, not through active RPG combat or party control.
**Dependencies:** RW02 timeline lifecycle, RF01 baseline and RF02 safe persistence; coordinate with RW00 pacing, RF03 rules and optional RW04/RW06/RW07 integrations.
**Implementation authorization:** None.

## Objective

Make Valley of Olympus a natural recurring location for **incremental boss encounters** across timelines. The player succeeds by improving stats and skills, accumulating resources, unlocking capabilities, and optimizing legal action queues over repeated inner time loops. Bosses may have phases, changing requirements and timeline-specific conditions, but the solutions are **incremental progression and planning**, not manual attacks, dodging, reflexes or a new party-management system.

Valley retains its separate gods/Restore Time climax; completing bosses **must not become an accidental requirement to drink the Looping Potion and enter Real World**. The selected shorter-Valley pacing direction remains governed by the [RW00 baseline measurement plan](balance-tests-implementation-plan.md).

## Source-based foundation

**Existing as-coded behavior:** Valley is zone 8. `Action.GodsTrial` (`GTrial`) contains **100 floors** using `getTeamCombat()`; `Action.ChallengeGods` (`GFight`) contains **seven floors** using `getSelfCombat()`. Both use the game's existing incremental trial/action systems and grant divine power. `Action.RestoreTime` uses the tower/power gate and legacy prestige award. See [live action definitions](../../../actionList.js) and the [potion/Valley analysis](potion-and-endgame-options.md).

These existing fights are a foundation, **not** an already implemented RW08 boss system. The old `getTeamCombat()` calculation is not approval or a dependency for any new RW05 party mechanic. Keep action IDs, `varName` values, old trial progress, nine town indices, credits, and prior player saves intact unless a later separately authorized migration explicitly changes them.

## Proposed player journey and first bounded slice

1. **Understand the encounter:** show the god, current phase, remaining incremental threshold, relevant stats/skills/resources, known modifiers and possible rewards in a compact view.
2. **Build a solution:** continue ordinary action-queue play to improve relevant capabilities. There should be more than one legal progression or preparation strategy; an automatic trial/action resolves the encounter when conditions are met.
3. **Learn from attempts:** give precise progress and bottleneck feedback so the player can adjust training, resources or queue efficiency across subsequent loops. Avoid requiring repeated identical waiting just to stretch the boss fight.
4. **Overcome distinct requirements:** start with **one adapted Valley boss** containing at least **two visibly different incremental phases or constraints**. Examples (not approved mechanics): a divine time check emphasizing Chronomancy/efficient schedules, or a resource-and-combat threshold emphasizing economic preparation.
5. **Record outcome once:** store deterministic boss progression, victory and any reward under explicit reset/retention rules. Later timelines may scale requirements or alter mechanics without paying the same one-time entitlement repeatedly.

Reuse the current action, trial, stats/resource, queue and predictor engines. Choose exact hooks only after characterizing legacy trial behavior. A typed/versioned boss ID, phase/rule definition and per-timeline outcome should be stable, serializable and recoverable. Boss tier/threshold curves, which gods appear and reward economics are **unresolved**.

**Optional future integrations:** [RW06 Timeline Potions](RW06.md) may impose boss modifiers; [RW07 Wagers](RW07.md) may stake on defeating or avoiding a boss condition. Those proposals remain **unimplemented and are not prerequisites** for RW08; use shared deterministic timeline rules when they exist. RW04 variants should not create a second boss rules engine.

## Explicit exclusions

No **party formation, companion control, group mechanics or RW05 implementation**. No real-time or turn-based battle engine, action minigames, timed input, enemy AI, manual dodging, multiplayer or boss-only UI framework. No new towns, removal of Valley, change to potion entry, wholesale challenge-mode rewrite, unlimited procedural boss generation or approval of new currencies/balance numbers.

## Dependencies and open design gates

- **Hard:** [RW02](roadmap.md) timeline identity and safe progression/outcome lifecycle; [RF02](../../refactoring/RF02-persistence-safety.md) recoverable save/import protection before changing persistent boss state.
- **Characterization:** [RF01](../../refactoring/RF01-regression-coverage.md) baselines for `GodsTrial`, `ChallengeGods`, power, `RestoreTime`, legacy floors/rewards and predictor differences; do not silently change current definitions.
- **Rules/performance:** [RF03](../../refactoring/RF03-gameplay-rules-and-resources.md) guides pure rule evaluation. At free ~10× replay speed, evaluation must stay bounded and match live/predictor worker results. [RW00 pacing](balance-tests-implementation-plan.md) provides evidence before changing time-to-boss targets.
- **Open product choices:** First god and phase design, whether boss victory is mandatory for the *original Valley ending*, lifetime of partial progress, per-timeline difficulty scaling, reward/recurrence limits and which optional potion/wager modifiers to allow. **Recommended default:** one non-reflexive, bounded encounter that preserves the original Valley reward path and makes no new Real World access prerequisite.

## Acceptance criteria

- **RW08-AC01:** A visible Valley boss clearly communicates its incremental preparation requirements, present phase/progress and potential reward; no manual attack input or party UI is necessary.
- **RW08-AC02:** At least two different legal improvements to ordinary stats, skills, resources or queued-action plans demonstrably change the boss result, without a separate combat simulator.
- **RW08-AC03:** At least two mechanically different incremental thresholds/phases produce deterministic transitions and concise actionable feedback after unsuccessful attempts.
- **RW08-AC04:** Boss configuration, progress and victory respect the approved ordinary-loop versus full-timeline reset boundaries, including reload, save export/import, interruption and rollback.
- **RW08-AC05:** Victory and rewards are granted at most once per eligible timeline/outcome; repeated attempts, duplicate callbacks and reloaded saves cannot farm the same award.
- **RW08-AC06:** Original `GodsTrial`, `ChallengeGods`, `RestoreTime`, Valley access, trial-floor identities and veteran saves remain compatible or have a separately accepted and tested migration.
- **RW08-AC07:** Boss eligibility, progress and rewards agree across the real game, foreground predictor and worker using the same versioned timeline configuration; invalid values fail safely.
- **RW08-AC08:** Boss progression stays meaningful without reintroducing a drawn-out Valley grind; attempt count, wall-clock pacing and CPU cost are evaluated against RW00 baseline and free ~10× throughput.
- **RW08-AC09:** A standard timeline can run bosses without RW05, RW06 or RW07; optional later modifiers/wagers are deterministic, never retroactively change committed outcomes and do not double rewards.
- **RW08-AC10:** The selected potion-to-Real-World gateway remains independent of Valley/boss completion; no changes to party gameplay are included or implied.
