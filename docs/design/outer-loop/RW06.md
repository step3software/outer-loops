# RW06 — Timeline Potions

**Status:** Proposed milestone; the potion identity is an owner-confirmed design direction (2026-11-07), while detailed mechanics remain proposed and unapproved.
**Repository:** `step3software/outer-loops`.
**Owner origin:** On 2026-11-06 the owner proposed Path of Exile-style keys that give a run different attributes and requested RW06; on 2026-11-07 the owner clarified that **keys are potions**: the original Looping Potion was one, and mastering its secret allows the protagonist to brew potions that give the **next run** chosen attributes.
**Sequence:** Follow-on to the initial Real World release; its numeric ID does not make RW05 a prerequisite.
**Implementation authorization:** None.

## Objective

Use **alchemical timeline potions** instead of abstract keys to configure the attributes of a new timeline. The player progresses from being affected by the original, uncontrolled Looping Potion to understanding its secret and deliberately creating new potions that change the conditions of subsequent runs. A potion modifies **one entire new timeline**, not just a single ordinary inner time loop. The choice should alter meaningful strategy, not merely raise numerical difficulty.

## Narrative and product identity — owner-confirmed direction

- The courier's original Looping Potion is the **first known example** of this class of time-altering alchemy, not a separate system from the future run modifiers.
- At a later stage, the protagonist understands enough of its underlying secret to **make their own timeline potions**.
- By altering a potion's formula or composition, the protagonist can deliberately give the **next run** different attributes.
- The Path of Exile map/key analogy remains a *design reference* for varied runs; **keys are not the player-facing object** in Outer Loops.
- This creates an explicit narrative through-line: **accidental entrapment → understanding the potion → mastering the conditions of new timelines**.

**Boundary with the existing game:** The current `Action.LoopingPotion`, its temporary `resources.loopingPotion` flag and the Jungle Totem/Wunderkind use are **as-coded behavior**, not an implemented RW06 potion inventory, formula system or timeline-start trigger. See [potion and ending analysis](potion-and-endgame-options.md). **Approved distinction:** Deliberately drinking the original Looping Potion to enter Real World without completing Valley is already approved (RW-D02). RW06 concerns **later custom-brewed potions for new timelines**; it does not implement first-entry behavior, settle original-potion access requirements or alter Valley's independent capstone. See [decisions](decisions.md).

## Player journey and proposed scope

1. Before a new timeline, the player can inspect any currently available **timeline potion formulas** and preview how each would change the run; a standard run without optional potion attributes remains a viable design path.
2. The player prepares or selects a potion whose effects are understandable in advance: benefits, drawbacks, affected systems, any special outcome conditions, resource requirements and possible rewards.
3. Starting the new timeline safely commits the potion's **effective, versioned modifier configuration** to that timeline. Normal inner-loop restarts, save/reload and export/import do not change or reroll its attributes.
4. The active timeline exposes a concise summary of its potion-imposed rules, and completion resolves any potion-specific outcomes/rewards once.

**Initial implementation scope (proposed):**
- A small curated set of distinct potion formulas illustrating at least two meaningful tradeoff domains, such as economy, progression or combat. These are design examples, **not approved recipes or balance numbers**.
- A data-defined contract connecting formula/composition identity to typed, stable modifiers, activation scope and deterministic conflict/precedence rules; runtime and predictor agree.
- Safe preparation/selection, eligibility checks, new-timeline commitment, restart handling and recovery without corrupting the prior timeline or duplicating resources.
- One coherent presentation of altered timeline rules alongside [RW04](roadmap.md), without reviving a second standalone Challenges menu.
- Timeline outcome/reward bookkeeping and a way to run a baseline timeline without optional modifiers.

**Out of scope for the initial version:** Fully procedural ingredient/affix generation, potion trading, elaborate brewing minigames, unrestricted rerolls or stacking, additional towns, parallel full-game simulation, wholesale rewriting of legacy challenges, or monetary purchasing. Future experiments may revisit these.

## Dependencies and unresolved decisions

- **Hard:** [RW02](roadmap.md) establishes safe timeline creation/completion and outer-state persistence; [RF02](../../refactoring/RF02-persistence-safety.md) supplies save/import/rollback safeguards before any potion resource can be reserved, consumed or committed.
- **Hard contract:** Share one deterministic variant-rule system with [RW04](roadmap.md) and the [Real World technical architecture](technical-architecture.md); RW04's implementation is not assumed complete.
- **Engineering:** Especially [RF03](../../refactoring/RF03-gameplay-rules-and-resources.md), maintain DOM-independent rules and foreground/predictor/worker equivalence; keep existing action identities and nine town indices.
- **Non-blocking:** RW05 multi-self management is not required. [RW07](RW07.md) can wager on an ordinary run or reference a committed potion-modified run.
- **Still open:** Where and when new formulas are learned; whether potions require ingredients and where they are acquired; whether formulas are reusable; whether brewing produces an item, whether or when it is consumed; the interaction between standard and customized potions; curated versus generated compositions; allowed combinations; rewards, difficulty and stacking; and how alternate selves participate. No specific recipe or currency is approved.

## Acceptance criteria

- **RW06-AC01:** The player can inspect each available potion's complete material attributes before committing to a new timeline and can access a standard run without optional modifiers; browsing does not affect the current timeline.
- **RW06-AC02:** At least two proposed potion formulas create observable, distinct strategic tradeoffs against the same standard baseline; effect and conflict evaluation is deterministic.
- **RW06-AC03:** A committed timeline records its potion/formula identity, definition version and effective modifier snapshot; inner-loop restart, refresh, export/import and predictor preserve identical effective rules.
- **RW06-AC04:** Invalid, incompatible or interrupted brewing/selection/commitment cannot duplicate or prematurely consume any potion-related entitlement, mutate the previous timeline, or leave an unrecoverable partial run.
- **RW06-AC05:** Completing a potion-modified timeline records its potion, outcome and approved rewards exactly once, including after re-evaluation, retry, revisit and reload.
- **RW06-AC06:** Standard unmodified play, original actions, base replay acceleration, existing timeline state and supported legacy saves continue to work.
- **RW06-AC07:** Potion-modified action eligibility and outcomes match between the actual game and predictor/worker in representative economy, time-gate and progression scenarios without unacceptable overhead.
- **RW06-AC08:** A compact interface communicates active potion attributes while keeping detailed effects accessible without cluttering the one-screen timeline/Real World view.

## Deliverables and verification

Produce a versioned formula/composition-to-modifier contract; a small testable sample potion catalog; a readable selection/brewing flow; a compatibility/conflict matrix; safe transaction, timeline reset, and reward semantics; and reproducible tests for standard and potion-modified timelines, malformed formula data, partial commitments, save recovery, inner-loop restarts, export/import, predictor parity and exactly-once rewards. Record evidence from actual runs when implemented. These are **future tests, not executed tests**.

**Stage boundary:** This is a source design/milestone specification only. Its approved potion *identity* is not approval of ingredient economics, action gating, implementation, merge or deployment.
