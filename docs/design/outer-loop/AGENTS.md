# Real World design-document instructions

Inherits [root AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) and [documentation AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

- This directory is **product/technical design**, not implemented mechanics. Label rules **Approved**, **Recommended / provisional**, **Open** or **Existing as-coded**. An accepted product direction is not an authorized source edit.
- Treat [decisions.md](decisions.md) as the decision record and [README.md](README.md) as the navigator. Update them when the user chooses a new behavior; avoid stale duplicate descriptions across files.
- Preserve the authoritative existing `RestoreTime` story, action definitions and nine-zone model. The Real World concept is not the jungle `OpenPortal` travel action or `townNum=9`.
- **Approved direction as of 2026-11-06:** **Drinking a genuine Looping Potion** enters a separate Real World version of Beginnersville; this **supersedes RW-D02's original post-`RestoreTime` portal gateway**. **Retain the full Valley of Olympus** as a separately meaningful, eventually shorter capstone after campaign-wide baseline tests. Do not remove zone 8. This is **not implemented**. The current potion is crafted pre-Valley with Spatiomancy normally reached via Valhalla and consumed at Jungle Totem for Wunderkind; design a distinct intentional drink/use and a fair shadow-path access route before changing gameplay. Never infer crafting solely from `storyMax`. The A/B/C study is retained as historical evidence, not an open choice to remove Valley.
- Do **not** resurrect dmchurch's Prestige Perks shop or old Challenges menu alongside Real World. Whether individual buffs, perks, challenges or old earned balances are *ported* remains an explicit open migration decision.
- A first deliberately started timeline **after potion arrival** gets initial **skill points**, a **substantial temporary boost** and free **~10× permanent replay throughput**. The points are not assumed to be old prestige points or an existing skill-point ledger; the boost's magnitude, duration and stacking remain open. Preserve the boundaries between temporary effects, wall-clock throughput, game-time actions, mana and predictor accuracy.
- First meeting: **several visible other selves** populate an alternate Beginnersville; **one notices the newcomer** and starts a short conversation. Allow exploration before explicitly starting a new timeline, without automatically deleting the **possibly unfinished** original-world/Valley state. Gradually introduce upgrade trees, automation, keys/wagers and **later party-based gameplay**. One character's exact identity, recruitment criteria and party mechanics remain proposals.
- The **working recommendation** for permanent abilities is [Will / Echoes / Anchors](upgrade-trees.md), with Anchors containing Memory/Treasury/Structures; retain the separate [Will / Echoes / Memory / Dominion alternative](dominion-alternative.md). Do not present either tree count, one-free-starter policy, node pricing, or exact first effects as finalized. Respect [upgrade contracts](upgrade-tree-contracts.md) and stable ability identity independent of display taxonomy.
- Any asset/art recommendations are references or intentions only. Do not generate pixel art or change visuals without an explicit image/artwork implementation request.
- All save and outer-state changes must coordinate with [RF01](../../refactoring/RF01-regression-coverage.md), [RF02](../../refactoring/RF02-persistence-safety.md), and [persistence audit](../../audit/persistence-contract.md). The refactoring milestone specs themselves remain approved **as plans** and are not implicitly reauthorized or revised here.
