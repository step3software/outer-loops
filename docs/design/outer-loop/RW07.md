# RW07 — Timeline Wagers

**Status:** Proposed milestone; owner requested its creation, not approval of wager economics or settlement rules.
**Repository:** `step3software/outer-loops`.
**Owner origin:** Conversation on 2026-11-06: allow a player to bet that a condition **will** or **will not** be met on the next run; subsequently requested a separate RW milestone.
**Sequence:** Independent follow-on to RW02; designed to combine with RW06 when customized timeline potions exist, but not to require them for ordinary runs.
**Implementation authorization:** None.

## Objective

Before committing to a new timeline, let a player make a meaningful, voluntary, in-game stake on a clearly defined prediction about that timeline's outcome. Both **will** and **will not** predictions are supported. A wager rewards deliberate strategic commitment rather than passive waiting, abandoning runs or gaming an ambiguous rule.

## Player journey and scope

1. In Real World, the player sees eligible predictions and a concise contract: target condition, positive or negative claim, qualifying success requirements, stake, potential in-game payout and behavior on failure/abandonment.
2. The player may take **no wager** or accept a wager before starting the **next** timeline. After the timeline starts, the contract cannot be edited, reinterpreted or staked retroactively.
3. A committed wager tracks only the relevant, authoritative gameplay events or end-of-timeline facts, using a stable versioned condition definition. A **will-not** condition pays only on an otherwise qualifying run, not because the player never played.
4. Completing or otherwise terminating the timeline settles the wager through a single durable outcome record. The UI explains success, failure or an unresolved/recoverable state without double-paying.

**Initial implementation scope (proposed):**
- A small, explicit catalog of positive and negative conditions, including at least one each for a reached milestone, a completion constraint and avoiding an action/event on a successfully completed timeline.
- A deterministic, versioned predicate and evidence model, tied to a single timeline ID and immutable pre-commit terms; record only the minimum useful facts.
- A reserved in-game stake, an auditable settlement result and an idempotent transfer/refund/loss mechanism compatible with safe save transactions.
- Compact selection, active-wager visibility, outcome and reward presentation; no obligatory betting to progress.
- Optional interaction with the active [RW06 Timeline Potion](RW06.md): a wager may reference the committed potion/formula or its effective rules, but potion modifiers must not silently alter locked wager terms.

**Out of scope:** Real-world money, cash-out, purchasable gambling tokens, external betting, tradable stakes, multiplayer wagers, NPC-backed financial systems, compound parlay engines and unrestricted player-authored code/predicates. Alternate-self wagers can be a future narrative presentation, not a prerequisite.

## Dependencies and gates

- **Hard:** [RW02](roadmap.md)'s explicit timeline start/end/outcome identity, and [RF02](../../refactoring/RF02-persistence-safety.md)'s safe persistent/transactional state and recovery.
- **Hard contract:** Timeline outcome/event definitions must be stable, deterministic and testable across ordinary loops, restarts, browser reloads and predictor-visible state. Reuse rules/contracts established through [RW04](roadmap.md) or [RW06](RW06.md) rather than a separate hidden game engine.
- **Soft:** RW06 potions enrich eligible conditions but are not required to wager on a standard run. RW05 alternate selves may eventually offer bets but are not required.
- **Owner decisions still open:** The in-game stake resource and payout model, one wager versus several, exact terminal/abandonment/refund policy, criteria eligibility, potion interactions and whether a narrative character is a wager counterparty. No currency, odds or value is approved.

## Acceptance criteria

- **RW07-AC01:** Before the next timeline starts, a player can decline or accept a wager with visible positive/negative prediction, settlement condition, stake, payout and noncompletion terms.
- **RW07-AC02:** A wager commits only to a new timeline, with immutable versioned criteria and the chosen stake reserved exactly once; inspecting or abandoning the selection screen does not charge the player.
- **RW07-AC03:** A completed qualifying timeline can correctly satisfy an affirmative **will** prediction and a negative **will not** prediction, using authoritative event/outcome evidence rather than a UI approximation.
- **RW07-AC04:** Merely not executing an action, never starting a timeline, pausing indefinitely, or intentionally abandoning an incomplete run cannot earn a negative wager payout.
- **RW07-AC05:** Success, failure and abandonment follow the explicitly approved settlement policy; reloading, duplicate completion events or interrupted transactions cannot create duplicate payouts, losses or refunds.
- **RW07-AC06:** The wager and its evidence survive inner-loop restart and supported save/reload/import; corrupt or unknown legacy wager records fail recoverably, without damaging a preexisting save.
- **RW07-AC07:** Wagers function on a standard run and, when RW06 is present, remain consistent with the committed potion's rule snapshot; the actual game and predictor agree on relevant conditions.
- **RW07-AC08:** Wager UI remains optional, concise and understandable; it distinguishes in-progress from settled outcomes and requires no real-world payment or account.

## Deliverables and verification

Prepare a versioned wager/condition contract, event/evidence and settlement model, economy decision record, a handful of representative positive/negative wager definitions, UI flow, failure/recovery matrix and deterministic tests for valid/invalid commitments, win/loss/abandonment, idempotent settlement, normal and potion-modified timelines, reload, old saves and predictor parity. Performance should use event-driven condition capture rather than full per-tick scanning. All tests are **future acceptance requirements**, not recorded passes.

**Stage boundary:** This is a source planning proposal only. It creates neither an Orcha stage receipt nor permission to implement, merge or deploy.
